## Reading List

<section class="notice">

**Last update: 08/10/26**

This is a reading list, containing books and documents that
I recommend to people. Value judgments not provided. List
in no particular order.

</section>

### Fiction

- Anything George Orwell. Seriously, anything. He wrote more than
*1984* and *Animal Farm*. *Down and out in Paris and London* and
*Homage to Catalonia* are excellent.

- Plato's *The Republic*. I like to grill people on an allegory
that appears in the book. You've heard of this allegory but not
many people know where it came from.

- *The Iliad* and *The Odyssey*. This funny thing called western
civilisation started here.

### Non-Fiction

- *Pragmatic Programmer* by Thomas and Hunt. It's *excellent*.

- *The Design of Everyday Things* by Don Norman.

### Important memos

- [Zero Defects](https://sriramk.com/memos/zerodef.pdf), Microsoft 1989.
Important for understanding code quality. This is from when most developers
could rightly call themselves engineers and understood operating systems.

- [Thoughts on
    Flash](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/web-design-history/steve-jobs-and-his-thoughts-on-flash-2010),
    Apple, 2010.
A very solid piece of business writing, designed to push a point without
being inflammatory.

- [How To Succeed In MrBeast
    Production](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6623b7720b009050313e701c/66ede69453b7bbadcd2f05a8_How-To-Succeed-At-MrBeast-Production%20(2).pdf).
It's a nice look behind the curtain. A bit sharp at times, but the general
gist is spot on, and offers insight into how a company became the very top
of the world at YouTube video production. Please read "Higher form of
communication".
